MoE Microscope: follow the data across the landscape

One wide scene, panned station by station in the order a forward pass happens. Chips move between stations, and every value on a chip is read from a pinned recording in the repository: nothing is computed in the browser and nothing moves that a recording does not hold. Stations without a recording yet are drawn empty and say so. The frame-by-frame page shows the same recordings as tables; the storyboard lists every scene, its source, and its acceptance rule.
