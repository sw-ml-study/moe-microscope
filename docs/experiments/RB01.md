# RB01: resource budget

## Question

How big is MicroMoE, what does one expert cost, and what do quantization,
residency, and transfer look like in numbers?

## Change from the previous experiment

No training. Parameter arithmetic per component, bytes at f64, FP32,
FP16, INT8, and INT4 payload, stored-versus-active ratios, and residency
and transfer estimates, each labeled M (measured), D (derived), or E
(estimate).

## Configuration

DN01, MX01, MX02, and LD01 shapes from the code; attention cost 4 d
squared; bandwidth figures from stated assumptions in the document.

## Results

| Quantity | Value | Label |
|---|---:|---|
| One full expert | 1,072 parameters, 8,576 bytes at f64, 536 bytes INT4 payload | D |
| Four-expert model at f64 | 55.4 KiB | D |
| Same with INT8 shared and INT4 experts, payload only | about 4.8 KiB | E |
| Stored to active, top-1 | 2.32 | D |
| Expert miss cost: HDD / SATA SSD / RAM | about 8 ms / 0.08 ms / 0.5 microseconds | E |
| Generated token, dense to top-2 | 0.16 to 0.5 ms | M (GB01) |

![RB01 storage: proportional bars of stored weights by component and the bytes one token touches under top-1 and top-2](../../assets/previews/budget-storage.svg)

## What we learned

At this scale storage latency, not bytes, is what a cache avoids; the
present four-expert model is fully resident, so TW01 needs a large bank
of padded shard records. The full document is
[resource economics](../results/resource-economics.md).

## What we did not prove

Any measured quantization loss or packed file size; QZ01 and PK01 replace
the estimates.
