CD01b docent word vectors. In-domain semantics without external data. scripts/assemble-campus-docs fetches the READMEs of the repositories linked from snapshot A (ibm-1130-rs, rca-1802-rs, sw-cdp1802-io, sw-cor24-apl, moe-microscope) once and writes fixtures/campus/docs-a.txt together with the snapshot's story and status sentences, taglines, and summaries (lowercased, punctuation stripped, one sentence per line; committed, so the gate is offline). lib/wordvec.mlpl trains word vectors on it deterministically: vocabulary of words with at least two occurrences, a co-occurrence matrix over a window of four, positive pointwise mutual information, and a rank-24 factorization by power iteration (or the sw-MLPL builtin if one fits; record the form in the catalog); nearest-neighbour lookup for diagrams. Store the vectors in an Engram-style hashed table addressed by ngram_hash over word ids (both forms if the builtin engram fits: from scratch and builtin, with parity), replacing the docent's random embedding rows; the docent fine-tunes them. demos/docent_wordvec.mlpl trains the same dense docent as CD01 on top of the table and measures everything CD01 measures, especially paraphrase destination and intent accuracy; diagrams of the co-occurrence matrix, nearest neighbours of a few words (switches, glyphs, disk), and the table. docs/experiments/CD01b.md reports CD01, CD01b, and both MB01 matchers side by side and states the margin. Gate script, catalog entry, tests over the vocabulary, PMI, factorization determinism, and the table lookup. If the margin over the stronger matcher is still under 20 points, say so plainly; do not tune on the paraphrase set.
